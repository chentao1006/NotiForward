NotiForward Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

NotiForward (知会 in Chinese) is a macOS menu bar app that forwards notifications you choose from your Mac to your iPhone through Bark. This policy explains what data the app handles and where it goes.

1. No Data Collected by the Developer

The developer does not collect, store, or receive any of your data. There is no account, no analytics, no tracking, and no server operated by the developer.

2. Notification Content

  • With your permission, NotiForward reads the macOS Notification Center folder (~/Library/Group Containers/group.com.apple.usernoted), which you select once in a system file dialog. It reads nothing else on your Mac.
  • Your forwarding rules (apps and keywords) are applied on your Mac. Notifications that do not match are never sent anywhere.
  • Notifications that match are sent over HTTPS to the Bark server you configure (by default api.day.app, operated by the Bark project), which delivers them to your iPhone through Apple Push Notification service. Use a self-hosted Bark server if you do not want notification content to pass through a third-party server.

3. App Icons

To show app icons in forwarded notifications, NotiForward looks up the sending app's bundle identifier (for example ru.keepcoder.Telegram) in Apple's public App Store lookup service. No notification content is included. This can be turned off in Settings.

4. Local Storage

Settings, your Bark key and a short-lived queue of notifications waiting to be sent are stored only on your Mac, inside the app's sandbox container. Queued notifications are deleted once sent, or after six hours.

5. Changes to This Policy

Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

6. Contact

Questions or concerns: github.com/chentao1006/NotiForward/issues

知会 隐私政策

隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 10 月

知会（NotiForward）是一款 macOS 菜单栏应用，通过 Bark 把你在 Mac 上选定的通知转发到 iPhone。本政策说明应用会处理哪些数据，以及数据去向。

1. 开发者不收集任何数据

开发者不收集、不存储、也不接收你的任何数据。没有账号，没有统计分析和追踪，开发者也不运营任何服务器。

2. 通知内容

  • 经你授权后，知会读取 macOS 通知中心文件夹（~/Library/Group Containers/group.com.apple.usernoted），该文件夹由你在系统文件对话框中选择一次。不会读取 Mac 上的其他任何内容。
  • 转发规则（应用和关键词）在你的 Mac 上执行，不符合规则的通知不会被发送到任何地方。
  • 符合规则的通知通过 HTTPS 发送到你设置的 Bark 服务器（默认为 Bark 项目运营的 api.day.app），再经 Apple 推送通知服务送达你的 iPhone。如不希望通知内容经过第三方服务器，请使用自建的 Bark 服务器。

3. 应用图标

为在转发的通知中显示应用图标，知会会把发送通知的应用的 Bundle ID（例如 ru.keepcoder.Telegram）提交给 Apple 公开的 App Store 查询服务，不包含任何通知内容。可在设置中关闭。

4. 本地存储

设置、Bark 密钥以及等待发送的通知队列仅保存在你的 Mac 上、应用的沙盒容器内。队列中的通知发送后即删除，最长保留 6 小时。

5. 政策更新

如有变更，将在本页面发布并更新日期。

6. 联系方式

如有疑问：github.com/chentao1006/NotiForward/issues
